## MLX Network: the Apple Silicon supercomputer

Macs everywhere, running AI together. Lend your Mac's spare time to the network and earn MLX Tokens for verified work.

[mlx.app](https://mlx.app) · [Live map](https://world.mlx.app) · [Explorer](https://explorer.mlx.app) · [What can your Mac run?](https://mlx.app/compare) · [Whitepaper](https://mlx.app/whitepaper) · [@MLXapp](https://x.com/MLXapp)

| Repository | What it is |
|---|---|
| [mac](https://github.com/MLXapp/mac) | MLX for macOS: the menu bar app that runs AI jobs for the network on Apple's MLX and earns MLX Tokens. |
| [ios](https://github.com/MLXapp/ios) | MLX.app for iPhone and iPad: watch and control your Macs from anywhere. |
| studio (coming soon) | [MLX Studio](https://studio.mlx.app): run, chat with and serve local AI models on your Mac. Built on vMLX. |

Everything here is open source under the Apache License 2.0. Official builds are signed and notarized, and published on [mlx.app/download](https://mlx.app/download).

MLX Network is independent and not affiliated with Apple. [Apple's MLX](https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx) is the machine learning framework our apps run on.
